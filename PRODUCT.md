# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Hiring managers, creative directors, and founders evaluating Chris for employment or freelance engagement. They arrive referred or via social, already hold 7+ tabs open, and make a first impression in under 10 seconds. They are culturally literate and can spot a template immediately.

**Secondary:** Potential collaborators and peers in the creative-tech space.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Chris Ma — Creative Technologist, Digital Marketer, and Product Manager — that converts a cold or warm visit into a conversation or offer. Success means the visitor leaves feeling inspired and certain they'd be in safe hands.

## Positioning

Chris works at the intersection of culture, technology, and commerce. Not purely a designer, not purely an engineer — someone who can hold the whole product and the cultural context it operates in simultaneously. That combination is the claim no neighboring portfolio can copy-paste.

## Operating Context

Visitors view this on desktop and mobile, often during a workday screening session. They scroll fast, look for signal over noise, and dismiss anything that reads as generic AI output. The portfolio competes against hundreds of similar Next.js/Tailwind builds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 (App Router), Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion — stack is fixed
- Sections: Hero, Work, About, Expertise, Process, Articles, Contact
- No headless CMS; articles likely static or fetched from a simple source
- Podcast audio assets in `/public/podcasts/`
- Must remain performant and accessible

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Chris Ma, monogram CM
- Voice: Confident, culturally sharp, not loud or hype-forward — earned authority
- No corporate, timid, or template-adjacent aesthetics
- Nothing that reads as AI-generated slop

## Evidence on Hand

- Work projects (content inside WorkSection — needs real case study data)
- Articles/notes (static or fetched)
- Podcast audio assets in `/public/podcasts/`
- Real copy: "Culture, technology, and commerce, applied. Building digital experiences that earn attention."

## Product Principles

1. **Earn the attention** — every section must justify why a fast-scanning visitor keeps scrolling
2. **Taste is the proof** — the design itself is the first portfolio piece; generic = disqualified
3. **Conviction over safety** — a bold wrong choice beats a timid correct one
4. **Work leads** — the artifact comes first; interface recedes
5. **No fabricated claims** — show what exists; mark gaps honestly
